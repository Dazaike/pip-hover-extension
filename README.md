# PiP Hover Button

Watch videos in a small, floating window while you browse other tabs or use other apps.

## Features

- **Picture-in-picture in one click:** Hover over a video and click the small button in its top-right corner.
- **Easy to close:** Click the button again to leave picture-in-picture mode.
- **Stays out of your way:** The button fades away when you're no longer hovering over the video.
- **Toolbar shortcut:** Click the extension's icon to turn picture-in-picture on or off for a video on the current page.

## Installation

1. Download `pip-hover-extension-v1.0.0.zip` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/Dazaike/pip-hover-extension/releases/latest) and extract it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome.
3. Turn on **Developer mode**, then click **Load unpacked**.
4. Choose the extracted folder.

## Good to know

The hover button may not appear on videos on some websites.
