## [v1.0.0] - 2026-10-12

Initial release (no prior history).

### Added
- Hover button: faint monochrome PiP icon at the top-right of any video rendered at 400x400 CSS px or larger; click toggles picture-in-picture.
- Fade in/out of the hover button.
- Toolbar action: toggles PiP on the largest video in the active tab (prefers playing videos).
- Extension icons (16/32/48/128).
